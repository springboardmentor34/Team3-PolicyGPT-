Overview

PM-KISAN is a Central Sector scheme launched to supplement the financial needs of landholding farmer families by providing income support of ₹6,000 per year in three equal installments. The amount is transferred directly to the beneficiaries' Aadhaar-linked bank accounts through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

check_circle

Eligibility Criteria

check_circle All landholding farmer families with cultivable land, subject to state-specific exclusions.
check_circle Family defined as husband, wife and minor children.
check_circle Valid Aadhaar-linked bank account required for Direct Benefit Transfer.
cancel Institutional landholders and income-tax payers are not eligible.
